# Direction — Governance that holds under pressure

**Question:** Does human control survive an agent that is smart, busy, and
under deadline pressure — without becoming day-to-day micromanagement?

Current state: `needsApproval` suspends named tools; a human resumes via
HTTP. Pause controls and the escalation protocol (Flow charter 03/04) are
unbuilt.

Open problems: approval fatigue (humans rubber-stamping), governance at the
plan level vs the action level, and the boundary between "escalate" and
"ask permission for everything". First experiment: measure approval rate and
fatigue under a fixed budget pressure.
